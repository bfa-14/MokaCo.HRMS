## Rules read from the procedures and used for the expectations

- **Grace** is per shift (`attendance.SHIFT.GraceMinutes`, Morning = 10, break 30, standard 510 min). `LateMinutes` = minutes after `ShiftStart + Grace` (grace subtracted). No setting deducts late minutes; pay follows `DayFraction = WorkedMinutes / StandardMinutes` where worked = last out − first in − break (early-in counted). `PunchDirectionMode = Alternate`, `PunchDebounceMinutes = 1`, `OvernightAttributionHours = 4`, `FullDayThreshold = 1.00`, `ExitLeaveBasis = Actual`.
- **Roster copy** (`usp_ShiftAssignment_CopyPeriod`) copies the most frequent shift per weekday, weekday-aligned; one-off days are not copied (R5b documents it).
- **Leave year** (`usp_LeaveYear_Open`): full tier by whole service years at 1 Jan; hired this year → `ROUND(tier × (13 − hire month) / 12 × 2, 0) / 2`; only the current year; idempotent. Verified: E1 21, E9 21, E8 (July) 7.5, E2 (August) 6.5.
- **Payroll** (`usp_PayrollRun_Generate`): coverage = employed **calendar** days / days in month, shown on the line as `Prorated 0.55 of the month`; DayRate = Basic / 26 (`StandardWorkingDaysPerMonth`); unpaid leave = days × DayRate; "Late Deduction" = Σ(1 − DayFraction) × DayRate over every record with DayFraction < 1 not inside approved leave; sick = year-to-date tiers; indemnity only from a prepared Separation request, never from `TerminationDate`; NSSF base = Salary/Overtime/Leave/Attendance lines in the primary currency; employee NSSF = Σ min(base, ceiling 2500) × EmployeeRate (3 %); employer = ceilinged schemes (8 % + 6 %) on min(base, ceiling) + EOSI 8.5 % on the full base; tax = brackets on (base − employee NSSF) × 12 − `TaxFamilyDeductionAnnualUsd` (absent → 0), / 12. Rates are snapshotted per run from `core.EXCHANGE_RATE` of the **configured** `PayrollRateType` (`NonOfficial`, 90000), not the Official rate.
- **Hand computation, E9 (P6)** with the correct LBP rate: base = 3500 + 6,000,000/90000 (66.67) − 673.08 (BUG-06) = 2893.59; employee NSSF = 2500 × 3 % = 75.00; employer = 2500 × 14 % + 2893.59 × 8.5 % = 595.96; annual taxable = (2893.59 − 75) × 12 = 33,823.08 → 2 % × 6000 + 4 % × 9000 + 7 % × 15000 + 11 % × 3823.08 = 1950.54 → 162.54/month. The proc produced 75.00 / 590.29 / 155.21: identical formulas on a base of 2826.92, i.e. with the LBP allowance counted as 0 (BUG-07). Employee NSSF matches only because the base is above the ceiling either way.
- **Bookings**: deposit % comes from the lead-time tier (≥168 h → 20 %, ≥48 h → 30 %, else 50 %) with a 5 floor; discount applies to the room price only, deposit to the discounted total including add-ons (B5: 60 − 6 + 15 = 69, deposit 13.80); guest cancel refund = paid − deposit (`BookingDepositRefundableOnGuestCancel = 0`).
- **Cultures (X3)**: the API binds query-string dates invariantly (`03/08/2026` is 8 March under en-US and en-GB alike) and the web app sends ISO `yyyy-MM-dd`, so both cultures agree.
