## Rules decided

Where the prompt was ambiguous, the rule written there was applied and the reading is recorded here.

1. **Permission windows.** A permission covers lateness / an early departure by its WINDOW: the approved minutes that overlap [shift start, first in) or (last out, shift end]. What is left of the approved total covers the mid-day gap and then, as before script 83, an early departure (HR's own approved figure has no window and stays pooled). A permission for another time of day does not excuse a late arrival. Each permission counts for no more than its approved minutes.
2. **What a permission covers is no anomaly at all.** Until script 83 the whole early departure was reported and auto-marked Excused; now only the uncovered part (if it reaches the tolerance) is shown to HR. The record still names the permission and carries the used minutes (`ExitLeaveMinutes` on the Actual basis) into the period-close conversion. Suite 1's A11d was re-targeted accordingly.
3. **Unrostered day = a day missing from an APPROVED roster month.** Only then is no record created. Where the month's roster is not approved (or does not exist) the roster is inert, exactly as before, and days keep their record on the default standard day — otherwise every real branch without an approved roster would stop producing attendance records. If the owner wants the stricter reading (no record without a roster, anywhere), it is one condition in `usp_Attendance_ComputeDay` (`@MonthApproved = 1 AND`).
4. **A decision answers a fact.** The same punches reprocessed — also with another tolerance or a newly approved permission — keep every decision (A1j). A punch that CHANGED (a correction, a late-arriving punch, the machine re-sending the day) withdraws an Excused / Deducted decision with a note that says what it was (A1k). `Corrected` rows are never touched.
5. **"The period is paid" is per employee** (D10): a locked primary run of the month that holds a payslip for THAT employee. A locked run says nothing about somebody it never paid; refusing their attendance would make it uncorrectable for ever (and real August is locked while both suites seed August data for their own employees). Automatic processing never raises on a paid day — it leaves the day as it was paid (`Outcome = 'Locked'`) and carries on; every HR-facing writer refuses with "This period is paid — raise a payroll adjustment instead."
6. **Tolerance in the suite** is changed through the QA2 shift's own `GraceMinutes` (NULL = the setting), never through the global `AttendanceToleranceMinutes` row, which is real data.
7. **Overtime for a past month** cannot be raised through `usp_Overtime_Create` (pre-approval is the rule, and it was kept). The fixture raises and approves it for a future date through the real procedures and moves that QA2 row's `WorkDate`.
8. **DST.** Punch times are the terminal's wall clock and shifts are built from `WorkDate + StartTime` with no zone, so both DST nights are complete wall-clock days with no anomaly. Only the autumn night's extra REAL hour is new logic: overtime within what an approved overtime request leaves.
9. **What a leave costs** (D2): the employee's rostered working days in the range. A rest day of their roster costs nothing (`LeaveCountsRestDays` = 0; 1 = every calendar day), a public holiday of their branch never does, and a day with no roster row counts as a working day (so an employee without a roster is charged calendar days, as before). A leave type with a FIXED entitlement (maternity: 70 days) is a span of the calendar and keeps counting calendar days. Attendance still marks every calendar day of the leave `Leave`.
10. **A partial approval keeps the first days.** The ledger is posted per month from the front of the range: approve 3 of 5 days over a month end and the first three working days are the ones used.
11. **The balance check** counts what is already promised to pending requests, applies to paid types that accrue, and is skipped for a discretionary leave TYPE. A discretionary GRANT at approval still works on any request that could be raised.
12. **"Retract / withdraw the same day"** is `usp_Request_RetractLastDecision` (the last signer, same day); `usp_Request_WithdrawDecision` is for a request that is still open and refuses a closed one by design.
13. **Carry-over expiry** treats usage as consuming the carried days first: unused = carried − this year's usage up to the expiry date. One 'Expiry' line per employee, type and year; shown with the adjustments in `vw_LEAVE_BALANCE` so the on-screen identity still adds up.
14. **A transfer has a date, and `EMPLOYEE.BranchId` means "the branch today".** A transfer dated ahead is recorded at once and becomes the current branch on its day (nightly `hr.usp_EmployeeBranch_ApplyDue`). It is refused before the hire date, before a later transfer already on file, and inside a period that is paid for the employee. Employees created before branch history existed were back-filled with one row: their branch since they were hired.
15. **An attendance record belongs to the employee's branch of that day**, not to the branch of the terminal that took the punch (the terminal is still in `DeviceId`). Otherwise somebody who keeps punching at the old branch's door after a transfer would stay in its reports.
16. **"A copy keeps the holidays"**: no shift is rostered on a public holiday of the employee's branch; a row already there is left alone. The day can never read as an absence (no roster row in an unapproved month = the roster is inert; in an approved month the day has no record unless it is worked, and then HR rosters it). `GenerateRange` and `ApplyPattern` are HR's explicit instruction for named days and were left as they are.
17. **Proration.** Each standing salary component is paid for its OWN days in the period: from the latest of (period start, hire date, its EffectiveFrom) to the earliest of (period end, termination date, its EffectiveTo), over the calendar days of the month. The line says "Prorated 15/31 of the month (0.48): 2026-08-01 to 2026-08-15". Day and hour rates come from the basic in force on the employee's last day in the period.
18. **The leave balance settled on termination is the ledger as it stands** on the payslip of the termination month, for paid types that accrue, at the day rate (basic / StandardWorkingDaysPerMonth). An employee hired and terminated inside the same year is paid the whole prorated grant of that year that is still unused — earned-to-date accrual does not exist in this system. When an approved separation settlement landing in the period already pays "Unused Leave Payout", the automatic line is not added. Like that line, it is outside the NSSF / tax base.
19. **Holiday work is wage-like** (inside the NSSF / tax base, like overtime); the premium is (HolidayWorkRate − 1) because the holiday itself is already paid. An UNPAID holiday (`IsPaid = 0`) costs whoever was rostered to work it one day.
20. **An advance instalment is capped per currency** at what is left of the payslip after every other line, oldest advance first; the remainder stays on the advance (its balance only ever moves at lock, by the amount on the line).
21. **Exit permissions beyond the leave balance** are recorded per day (`ATTENDANCE_RECORD.ExitUnpaidDays`) when the period is closed, and deducted by the payslip as "Unpaid Leave Deduction". On a day already paid, the remainder is simply not converted.
22. **Refusal cases inside a rolled-back transaction go last.** A refusal (RAISERROR) caught in a transaction whose procedure SET XACT_ABORT ON leaves it uncommittable; reads still work, so the remaining refusals can still be asked for, in an order that puts procedures that write a temp table first.
23. **D9 quarantine is a view**, `attendance.DEVICE_PUNCH_QUARANTINE`, over the punches `usp_RawLog_Insert` already stores with no employee. A second table would be a second copy of the same punches.

2. **"Unrostered day"** (rule 12) only applies inside an APPROVED roster month. In a month with no approved roster the day is derived from the employee's default pattern as before. The owner may want it stricter.

5. **The roster page** has no branch filter of its own; the API takes `branchId` (as-of the work date) but the page does not send it yet.
